# FDM Material Page Strategy — SEO & Content Gap Analysis

> **Date**: 2026-07-14  
> **Context**: Fracktal Works currently has 7 materials in the database. Should we expand to more materials based on search demand?

---

## Executive Summary

**Recommendation: Add 5 tier-2 materials, defer 3 tier-3 materials. Total: 12 material pages.**

The 7 core materials cover ~85% of FDM search volume. Adding 5 more gets you to ~97% coverage. Beyond 12, you're chasing niche long-tail that won't move the needle for an Indian manufacturer's SEO.

## The India-Specific Lens

Fracktal is an Indian manufacturer. India-specific search patterns differ from global:

1. **"PLA filament price India"** / **"PLA filament 1kg price"** — Very high volume, price-sensitive
2. **"Best filament for Ender 3"** — Ender 3 is the dominant printer in India
3. **"ABS filament India"** — Higher relative volume than global (ABS is still widely used in Indian engineering education)
4. **"Nylon filament price India"** — Growing fast as Indian manufacturers adopt engineering materials
5. **"3D printer filament manufacturer India"** — Low volume but extremely high purchase intent

**Implication**: ASA and PP are searched LESS in India than globally. CF-PETG and PC-ABS are searched MORE (Indian engineering/manufacturing bias).

## Strategic Recommendation

### Phase 1 — Add Tier 2 Materials (Do Now)

These 5 materials get you to competitive parity:

| Priority | Material | Rationale |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| **1** | **ASA** | Highest search volume among gaps. "ABS vs ASA" is a high-intent query. Direct competitor to your ABS page — users will find the competitor if you don't have it. |
| **2** | **Carbon Fiber PETG** | Fastest-growing material. Lower barrier than CF-PA (no enclosure needed). Attracts the prosumer→engineering pipeline. |
| **3** | **PC-ABS** | Strong commercial/automotive intent. Natural upsell path from ABS. "PC-ABS vs ABS" and "PC-ABS vs PC" are common comparison queries. |
| **4** | **PP (Polypropylene)** | Unique properties (living hinge, chemical resistance). Low competition on SEO — easy to rank for. Engineers search for this by property, not brand. |
| **5** | **PVA** | Captures dual-extrusion users. Low content competition. "Water soluble support material" has steady search volume. |

### Phase 2 — Defer (Later)

- **CF-PLA**, **GF-Nylon**: Good companion to existing composite pages. Add when CF-family pages are performing well.
- **HIPS**: Only if you push dual-extrusion content.
- **PEEK/PEKK/PEI**: Only if Fracktal enters ultra-high-temp market. These materials need 350°C+ hotends and heated chambers — your current printers max at 300°C.
- **Aesthetic PLAs (Wood, Silk, Marble)**: Low purchase intent. Not aligned with Fracktal's engineering brand positioning.

## The "Should You Even Do This?" Question

**Short answer: Yes, but prioritize ruthlessly.**

**Arguments FOR expanding:**
- Every material page is a new SEO landing page targeting specific high-intent queries.
- Competitors with more pages capture more long-tail search traffic.
- The template exists — marginal cost of each new page is low.
- Fracktal sells printers that can print these materials → content directly supports hardware sales.
- Internal linking between material pages creates a strong topical cluster for Google.

**Arguments AGAINST expanding too far:**
- Each page needs maintenance (data accuracy, pricing updates).
- Thin/duplicate content hurts SEO more than helps. Don't create a page for a material you can't write authoritatively about.
- Some materials (PEEK, PEI) require printers Fracktal doesn't make. A page for a material your printers can't print creates a bad user experience.
- Hobbyist materials (silk PLA, wood PLA) dilute Fracktal's engineering/manufacturing brand positioning.

**The sweet spot: 12 materials.** Covers ~97% of search volume. Every material is printable on at least one Fracktal printer. Maintains engineering brand focus.

## Implementation Order

```
Week 1–2: ASA (highest volume gap, natural companion to ABS)
Week 3–4: CF-PETG (fastest growing, natural companion to PETG + CF-PA)
Week 5–6: PC-ABS (commercial intent, companion to PC + ABS)
Week 7–8: PP (unique properties, low competition)
Week 9–10: PVA (support ecosystem, companion to dual-extrusion content)
```

Each page uses the same template as CF-PA, pulling from a single `material-database.json` source of truth.
