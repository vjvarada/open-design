## Key Findings

### 1. ASA is MASSIVE globally, invisible in India
- **Worldwide**: 64 (top of Batch B engineering group)
- **India**: 2 (near-zero)
- **Implication**: ASA demand is almost entirely US/EU/Australia. If Fracktal targets global customers, ASA is essential. If India-only, skip it.

### 2. "Carbon fiber nylon" is the right keyword — NOT "carbon fiber filament"
- "Carbon fiber filament" (India): 1 ⬅️ dead term
- "Carbon fiber nylon" (Worldwide): 27 ⬅️ strong, real volume
- **Difference**: 27x. The compound keyword matters enormously.
- **Verdict**: CF-PA page was the correct lead. Target "carbon fiber nylon" in SEO.

### 3. "Nylon filament" is the top related query for "carbon fiber nylon"
- People searching CF nylon also search regular nylon
- Internal linking between Nylon PA12 and CF-PA pages = SEO gold

### 4. PETG + CF = Powerful combination
- PETG at 26 (Batch A) is the #2 material globally
- CF-PETG at 9 (Batch B) is the #2 composite
- Combined, PETG + CF-PETG covers a massive audience

### 5. Polycarbonate and PP are tied
- Both at 8 in Batch B
- PC has higher purchase intent (industrial)
- PP has lower competition (easier to rank)
