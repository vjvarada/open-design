Since 2013

Founded by Engineers, for Engineers

Fracktal Works was founded in 2013 by Rohit Asil and Vijay Raghav Varada, engineering students at the Manipal Institute of Technology working with the Robomanipal robotics team. Building their own robots meant they needed 3D printers they could actually afford — so they built their own.

That printer became a company. Over a decade later, Fracktal designs and manufactures every machine itself, from compact desktop systems to large-format industrial printers.

FAQ

Common Questions

Is Fracktal Works an actual 3D printer manufacturer, or a reseller?

Fracktal designs, engineers, and manufactures every printer at its own facility in Bangalore. We are an Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) — we do not import and rebadge machines.

Where are Fracktal 3D printers made?

Every Fracktal printer is designed and assembled in Bangalore, India. Motion systems, electronics, and firmware are developed in-house.

What materials can Fracktal printers use?

Depending on the model, Fracktal printers support ABS, ASA, PLA/PLA+, PETG+, TPU, polycarbonate, Nylon 12, Nylon CF, and other composite filaments. See individual product pages for the full list per machine.

Does Fracktal offer industrial-scale 3D printing, or only desktop machines?

Both. Snowflake and Julia are desktop-class machines; Twin Dragon scales up to a 600 × 600 × 400 mm build volume for larger, production-oriented parts.
